Attention Is All You Need

Vaswani et al., 2017 — presented by Karneeshkar

The claim

Drop recurrence. Keep attention.

The whole encoder-decoder stack is attention plus feed-forward layers, and it trains faster than the recurrent models it replaces.

Why it holds up

Three properties

Parallel

All positions are computed at once. Sequence length no longer serialises training.

Short paths

Any two tokens are one hop apart, so long-range signal does not decay through steps.

Scalable

The same block repeats. Capacity grows with width, depth and data, not with new machinery.
